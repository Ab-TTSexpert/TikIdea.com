FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I appeal a TikTok Shop violation?

We read the violation notice, check your Seller Center evidence, and write a truthful appeal with the right documents (invoices, authorisation, order proof). For IPR and counterfeit flags we also check brand authorisation first. TikTok makes the final decision, so the appeal has to be accurate and complete.

Why was my TikTok Shop suspended or deactivated?

The common causes are IPR or counterfeit flags, brand authorisation gaps, fake or low-quality orders, repeated policy violations, and account health score drops. We review the reason in Seller Center, fix the root cause and prepare the reinstatement appeal.

How do I get brand approval or category approval on TikTok Shop?

We prepare the brand authorisation and documents TikTok asks for, check they match your shop details, submit them and follow up through support cases until approval is decided. This covers restricted categories and brand authorisation for resellers.

Why is my TikTok Shop listing rejected or stuck in review?

Usually the title, images, category, prohibited-product rules or missing documents. We rewrite titles, descriptions, highlights and search terms and fix the compliance issue so the listing can pass review.

How do I get more affiliate creators on TikTok Shop?

We build the affiliate programme: finding creators that fit your products, setting commissions and campaigns, running creator communities on WhatsApp and Discord, and adding retainers and incentives so creators keep posting.

Do you run TikTok Shop ads and GMV Max?

Yes. We set up and manage GMV Max and Shop Ads against your margins, watch ROI daily, and scale what works.

How long until I see results?

It depends on the shop, but the usual pattern is groundwork in the first weeks, first traction within about a month, and clearer growth in months 2 and 3. We share progress in regular reports.

What if results are slower than expected?

We review the numbers with you, find what is holding the shop back (creators, content, listings, ads or account health) and change the plan. Plans are monthly, so you are never locked in.

Which platforms do you integrate with?

Shopify, Amazon, WooCommerce and common ERP tools, using connectors such as Silk Connector or Shopify's built-in TikTok app, depending on your setup.

Do you guarantee approvals or sales?

No one can. TikTok makes the final decision on approvals, appeals and reinstatements, and sales depend on your product, price and market. What we guarantee is honest work, complete and accurate submissions, and clear reporting.

How do you get access to my shop?

You add us as a user from Seller Center with an email on our own domain. We never ask for your password.
