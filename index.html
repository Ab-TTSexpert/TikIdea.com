Problems we solve

Every stage of the seller journey, covered

From the first signup to scaling sales, most shops are held back by one or two clear problems. We find them first, then fix them.

1

Signup and verification

  • Signup rejected or stuck
  • Business or ID verification failed
  • Wrong region or business type selected
  • Seller Center access and sub-account issues

We review what was submitted, find the mismatch and guide the correct resubmission.

2

Brand and category approval

  • Brand authorization rejected
  • Restricted category locked
  • Invoice or document rejections
  • Application stuck in review

We fix the rejection reason, resubmit and follow up through support cases. Real documents only.

3

Listings

  • Listing failed or rejected
  • Product stuck in review or frozen
  • Pricing, SKU and image issues
  • Low visibility and low conversion

Better titles, descriptions, highlights and search terms, built to pass review and sell.

4

Violations and account health

  • Violation points and warnings
  • Failed or rejected appeals
  • Counterfeit and IPR flags
  • Deactivated shop, LIVE and listing restrictions
  • Fake order and low-performance flags

Evidence-based appeals and a recovery plan, plus a checklist so it does not return.

5

Sales and growth

  • Sales dropped or never started
  • No creators, or creators not posting
  • Affiliate sample orders not converting
  • Ad spend with weak returns on GMV Max

Audit first, then affiliate, creator and ads plans focused on profit.

6

Daily operations

  • Tracking and shipping violations
  • Late shipping, cancellations and returns
  • Orders, customer support and reports
  • No time to run the shop

We run it daily on a fixed monthly plan, with clear reporting.

TikTok makes the final decision on approvals and appeals. We make sure every submission is complete, accurate and honest.
